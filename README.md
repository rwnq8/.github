# Rowan Brad Quni-Gudzinas

**Autonomous AI systems researcher.**

---

I study autonomous AI systems as systems: how their parts interact, where they
fail, and what keeps them legible and accountable to the people who rely on
them. I am a systems researcher, not a software engineer. My work is system
design, modeling, evaluation and oversight.

That work grows out of two decades of network modeling and data-driven decision
systems: transportation network modeling from my early jobs in
transportation planning, specifying the U.S. Federal Highway
Administration's national Tour-Based Model System and co-directing the $10M
National Household Travel Survey, analytics and machine
learning at Deloitte, and the AARP Livability Index.

---

## QNFO: autonomous systems research

**[github.com/QNFO](https://github.com/QNFO)** is the home of
[QNFO](https://qnfo.org), the independent research imprint I founded in 2024.
It operates an autonomous AI research system that drafts, referees, revises,
publishes and corrects papers, and studies that system: what it delivers, what
it costs, how it fails, and how its claims stay auditable. See
[Operating the Quniverse Fleet](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23079905) and
[Epistemic Legibility in AI-Assisted Science](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22026592).
